## 5. The System — `02 — THE SYSTEM`

- Headline: **One focused shoot can power your whole month.**
- Intro: We plan the stories before the camera arrives, so every minute of capture produces usable content.

Four steps:

1. **Strategy call** — Offers, priorities, audience and content angles.
2. **Planned shoot** — People, products, demonstrations and customer moments.
3. **Month of assets** — Reels, graphics, captions and platform variants.
4. **Clear scorecard** — What worked, what changed and what comes next.

Supporting facts used inside the step illustrations (not headline copy):
- A month is planned as 4 weeks × 7 days.
- One shoot day yields a 12-shot list across the four categories People, Products, Demonstrations, Customer moments.
- The month of assets is 12 reels + 12 graphics, each reel with platform variants for Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
- The scorecard sorts content into **Keep · Stop · Test · Amplify**.

Closing line: **One strategist. One calendar. One approval window.**
(The approval window is 48 hours.)
